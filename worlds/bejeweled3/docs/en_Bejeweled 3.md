## What does randomization do to this game?

Game modes and gating items (Relic Keys, Progressive Level Caps, Progressive Badge Tiers) come from the multiworld.
Reaching Classic levels, earning badges and completing quests send checks.

## What is the goal?

Configurable: collect Jewel Shards, reach Classic level 10 (Levelord), or complete Quest mode (Heroes Welcome).
